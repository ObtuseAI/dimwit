# Contributing

Dimwit accepts focused issues and pull requests for its public research
preview.

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `LICENSE`, and the affected capability or
   promotion policy.
2. Describe the production outcome, engine/tool boundary, mutation scope,
   evidence contract, rollback, and human-review impact.
3. Never weaken a validator or raise the autonomous ceiling above
   `PROMOTED_TO_REVIEW`.
4. Prefer integration, engine-adapter conformance, recovery, supply-chain, and
   smoke proof.
5. Run `python scripts/pipeline/run_validation.py`; preserve honest `BLOCKED`
   states for unavailable engines, assets, providers, or human visual review.
6. Do not include credentials, private game assets, workstation paths,
   proprietary third-party content, store/account data, or fabricated proof.

Submitting a contribution indicates agreement with the contribution terms in
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
